# SonderKey 4.5

## Autopilot

- **"Grow the keys" can now actually be seen.** The growth setting was capped low enough that a favoured letter only ever expanded into the gap beside it and stopped there, which read as a very slightly larger key. It now goes far enough for the expected letter to sit over the keys next to it, so what the dictionary is betting on is obvious without looking for it. Existing settings are unchanged in meaning: the slider still runs 1 to 10, each step is simply worth more.
- **A grown key is drawn above its neighbours on both sides.** Previously it was drawn above the key to its left and beneath the one to its right, which was invisible while the growth was small and lopsided once it was not.

Autopilot itself is still off by default, and what your finger actually hits has not changed.
