# SonderKey 4.4

## Voice typing

- **Numbers are written as digits.** "twenty five" becomes 25, "three hundred and forty two" becomes 342. Words that are only sometimes numbers are left alone: "one of them" and "the one I meant" stay as they were. Can be turned off.
- **Punctuation is removed by default.** Dictated text arrives without commas, full stops or question marks, which suits messages, searches and lists. Apostrophes and hyphens are kept, so "don't" and "well-known" survive. Turn the setting off to keep the recogniser's punctuation.
- **"Fit dictated text to its surroundings" now really means off.** With it off, the capital and full stop the recogniser adds to every phrase are dropped as well. Dictating a single word gives you that word, not "Word." — which also settles the long-standing complaint about excessive full stops in *At every pause*.
- **Keep correcting no longer rewrites text it already got right.** It used to re-transcribe the whole dictation every second, so a word understood correctly early could be replaced by a worse reading later. Speech that sits behind a pause and has several seconds of talking after it is now settled and left alone; only the most recent few seconds are reconsidered.
