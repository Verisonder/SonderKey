# SonderKey 4.3

## Autopilot

A new option at the top of Text correction, off until you turn it on.

As you type, the keyboard works out which letters could come next and quietly widens their touch areas at the expense of their neighbours. Type "th" and the targets for e, i and a grow. Nothing on screen moves, nothing you have already typed is ever changed, and no word is silently rewritten - the only thing that changes is where the boundary between two keys sits at the moment your finger lands.

- **Strength** decides how far a boundary may move. It is deliberately capped: a press anywhere near the middle of a key is never overridden, however confident the dictionary is, and space, backspace, shift and enter are never touched at all.
- **Grow the keys** draws the expected letters larger as well, so you can see what is being favoured rather than only feeling it.
- **Show boundaries** outlines the widened areas on the keyboard, for working out what strength suits you.

## Build channel

- The build channel now has a refresh button, so a newer build can be checked for without leaving the screen and entering the code again.
