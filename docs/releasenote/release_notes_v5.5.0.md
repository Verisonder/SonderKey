# SonderKey 5.5.0

## Private mode, stronger

- **A private clipboard.** While private mode is on, anything you copy is taken off the phone's clipboard and kept only inside the keyboard, so other apps and websites can't read it. Paste it from the keyboard's clipboard, the paste key or the clipboard suggestion. Your normal clipboard history is kept apart and isn't shown or touched.
- **Deleted on its own.** Each private copy is removed 5 minutes after you copy it, and everything is wiped the moment you leave private mode. Nothing private is ever saved to storage or backups.
- **Online AI pauses.** Proofreading, translation and custom AI keys send nothing while private mode is on. The offline translation plugin still works.
- Note: in private mode the app's own Paste button finds nothing — paste through the keyboard.

## A new look for settings

- The settings app now shares the Verisonder look: a black page, electric blue, bold headings, accent section labels and icons, and the swoosh on the main page. The keyboard's own colours are unchanged.

## Privacy and fixes

- **Passwords copied from a password manager are never saved** to clipboard history.
- Screenshots are not added to clipboard history while private mode is on.
- Fixed: tapping a clipboard item that had just expired or been cleared could close the keyboard.
- Fixed: clearing the whole clipboard history reported the wrong count to the panel.
